Creative writers’ experience of self-determination: An examination within the grounded theory framework

Enko, J.

Free PDF (accepted manuscript) Published version · DOI

Abstract

The aim of this study was to apply grounded theory methodology to perform an examination of creativity and self-determination among writers. Four amateur creative writers were interviewed on the subject of their creative experience. Applying a grounded theory approach revealed a novel middle-range theory of the role of autonomy in artists’ creativity. The results indicated that self-determination and autonomy are necessary, yet not sufficient, conditions for creative writing. A model for self-determination of amateur creative writers was built with autonomy as the core category and authorship beliefs and autonomous regulation of the creative process as more distal components. The results suggest that autonomy-supportive surroundings may have a beneficial and facilitating influence on developing these writers’ creative talents. This model serves as a tool facilitating creative performance of amateur creative writers and can be considered in the process of designing a creativity-fostering environment for gifted individuals.

Keywords: autonomy, creativity, creative writing, self-determination, grounded theory
