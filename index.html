Research

I study creativity and positive psychology using psychophysiology and psychometrics. My current National Science Centre project tests how social power and cardiovascular challenge and threat responses shape creative problem-solving. I also adapt and validate questionnaires, and I bring a psychophysiological and psychometric perspective to collaborative projects, from positive emotions to academic dishonesty and moral emotions.
