Determining antioxidant interactions in various analytical systems: A methodology integrating antioxidant activity assays, isobologram analysis and interaction indexes

Gliszczyńska-Świgło, A., & Enko, J.

Free PDF (accepted manuscript) Published version · DOI

Abstract

Knowledge of possible interactions between antioxidants can be useful for the food and cosmetics industry to design products with more favourable antioxidant properties or oxidative stability. It is therefore crucial to know how to identify the type of interactions observed. In this study, we present a practical procedure for determining the interactions of antioxidants in different analytical systems, including an adaptation of isobologram analysis that allows its use to result obtained in assays such as Trolox equivalent antioxidant capacity (TEAC) and ferric reducing antioxidant power (FRAP). Using extracts of green tea (GTE), red tea (RTE), grape seed (GSE) and green coffee (GCE) as examples, their interactions with ascorbic acid (AcA) in relation to their antioxidant activity are presented. The antioxidant activity of extracts, AcA, and their mixtures, in the ratios of 2:1 and 1:2, was determined using TEAC with ABTS•+, FRAP, and 2,2-diphenyl-1-picryl-hydrazyl (DPPH) methods. The oxidative stability of oil-in-water emulsions containing extracts, AcA and their mixtures was also measured using the accelerated oxidation method (RapidOxy). A methodology for determining the type of interactions is presented, including isobolograms and interaction indexes (IID) calculated differently depending on the test. Because antioxidant capacity assays report activity rather than dose–response effects, a transformation of assay outputs was introduced to allow construction of isobolograms and calculation of IID. The practical examples, including Excel template, presented in this article may be helpful for those planning to study interactions between antioxidant components of mixtures as there is still a lack of research in this area.

Keywords: antioxidant, interaction index, isobologram, antagonistic effect, synergistic effect, product design
